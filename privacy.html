Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how indianrailwaytrainenquiry (the "Site," "we," "us") handles information when you visit and use our train and station enquiry tool. We built this site as a free reference for railway enthusiasts, train spotters, and travellers, and we keep data collection to the minimum necessary to operate it and support it with advertising.

1. Information We Collect

This Site does not require account creation, login, or any personal submission to use. We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers through the Site itself.

Like most websites, we automatically receive certain technical information when you visit, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, typically city or region level)
  • Pages visited and time spent on the Site
  • Referring website (the page you came from)

This information is collected automatically through standard web server logs and analytics, and is used only to understand how the Site is used and to improve it.

2. Cookies and Advertising

This Site displays advertisements served by third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense and other ad partners. These networks may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites, in order to provide advertisements about goods and services that may interest you.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this Site and other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendor cookies through the Digital Advertising Alliance.

We do not control the cookies set by these third-party advertising networks. Please review the privacy policies of Google and any other ad networks shown on this Site for more detail on how they use data.

3. Train and Station Data

The schedules, train numbers, station codes, and route information displayed on this Site are sourced from publicly available railway datasets. We do not collect or store any personal travel data, booking information, or PNR details. This Site does not connect to IRCTC or any official Indian Railways booking system, and no personal travel information is ever transmitted to or from this Site.

4. Third-Party Links

This Site may contain links to external websites, including official Indian Railways resources, IRCTC, or other railfan communities. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any third-party site you may visit through a link on this Site.

5. Children's Privacy

This Site is a general-audience reference tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this Site, please contact us so we can address it.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited technical data this Site processes, but no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how this Site operates, please visit our Contact page.
